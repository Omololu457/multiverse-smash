HOW THE INPUTS WORK
-------------------
FULL-KIT characters use SUMMON-THEN-SELECT:
  1. Stage a clone formation:   down, down, up  +  Special   ( ↓↓↑ + Special )
  2. Within the ~1 second window, press Special AGAIN with a held direction
     (or press Ultimate) to pick one of the 6 moves.

LIGHT-KIT characters (Sasuke, Obito) use a DIRECT TRIGGER:
  ↓↓↑ + Special with a held direction fires the move immediately (no formation).

BUTTON MAP  (Player 1 keyboard;  gamepad in [brackets])
  Down  = S            Up = W
  Special = L          [ Triangle / Y ]
  Ultimate = U         [ L2 / R2 ]
  Forward / Back       = toward / away from the opponent  (facing-relative)

SELECT DIRECTIONS (full kit) — pressed AFTER the summon, within the window
  Pure Attack  = (neutral) + Special
  Grab         = Forward   + Special
  Ranged       = Back      + Special
  Defensive    = Up        + Special
  Deception    = Down      + Special
  Swarm        = Ultimate  (while the formation is staged)

Notation below:  "↓↓↑+L -> L"  means  summon (↓↓↑+Special)  ->  select (press L).
Keyboard is first, gamepad second.

========================================================================
NOTE — IN-GAME MOVE LIST SCREEN
========================================================================
These per-character move NAMES do NOT appear on the in-game Move List screen
(its kit panel is driven by getKit() in kits.js, a separate system from
cloneChoreography.js). The Move List's "Controls" tab (and the pause Controls
overlay) DO show the generic input:
  "Clone Summon — ↓↓↑ + Special"  +  "...then hold a direction + Special to pick:
   Neutral=Pure Attack · Down=Deception · Up=Defensive · Back=Ranged · Fwd=Grab · Ultimate=Swarm"
...but not the individual named moves above.

NARUTO uses his own dedicated engine (narutoChoreography.js), but as of the
2026-10-08 pass he is on the SAME roster-standard summon-then-select scheme:

  Summon          ↓↓↑ + Special          (roster-standard; alternates kept: Up+Special, ↓↑+Special)
  Pure Attack     Uzumaki Barrage        ↓↓↑+L -> L      (also direct: Down+Special, or ↓→↓→)
  Ranged          Shadow Clone Shuriken  ↓↓↑+L -> Back+L
  Defensive       Substitution Escape    ↓↓↑+L -> Up+L
  Grab            Clone Flank Strike     ↓↓↑+L -> Fwd+L
  Swarm           Two Thousand Combo     ↓↓↑+L -> Ultimate   (while the formation is staged)
  (Naruto has no Deception sequence — a Down-select falls back to Uzumaki Barrage.)

  ULTIMATE = NINE-TAILS (Kurama Avatar / Tailed Beast Bomb):
    Neutral Ultimate  = Nine-Tails        (alternates: Down+Ultimate, or ↓←↓←+Ultimate)
  Other specials unchanged: Dark Rasengan = Back+Special (or ↓←↓←); Chakra-Arm Strike = Fwd+Special (or ←↓→).

========================================================================
SASUKE DŌJUTSU PASS — MANGEKYŌ / RINNEGAN SPACE-TIME
Applies to ALL THREE Sasukes:  SASUKE (original) · SASUKE (adult) · SASUKE (sensei)
========================================================================

EXTRA BUTTONS (on top of the BUTTON MAP near the top of this file)
  Charge = P        (a HELD modifier — hold it, then press Special)
  Block  = ;  (semicolon)

The dōjutsu live on Sasuke's previously-unused CHARGE modifier + on blocking,
so they never replace any of his normal specials (plain Special still works).

SHARED RINNEGAN SPACE-TIME MOVES  (identical inputs on every Sasuke; the gating differs per character — see below)
  R1  Amenotejikara: Kunai Swap     Charge + Special          (1st press = THROW a marker;
                                                                2nd press = SWAP places with the marker,
                                                                or with the foe if the marker is near them)
  R2  Portal Chidori                Charge + Forward + Special
  R5  Portal Redirect               Charge + Back + Special    (opens a ~0.7s window that REFLECTS a projectile)
  R3  Counter Swap                  Special — the instant AFTER you BLOCK a hit (i.e. during blockstun):
                                    warps you BEHIND the attacker with brief i-frames
  R4  Rinnegan Strain               (PASSIVE — no input; firing the space-time moves back-to-back
                                     overloads the Rinnegan and LOCKS it for ~2.5s. Pace yourself.)

MANGEKYŌ
  M1  Kagutsuchi Flame Control      while your Amaterasu flame is on screen:
                                      hold Up / Down      = STEER the black flame up / down
                                      re-press Amaterasu  = SNUFF it out (+ small chakra refund)

------------------------------------------------------------------------
SASUKE  (original / Mangekyō-era — the one with Susanoo)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Rinnegan suite (R1 / R2 / R3 / R5)  — works ANY time, no setup. Inputs exactly as the shared list above.
  M1 Kagutsuchi  — N/A: this Sasuke has no Amaterasu move in his kit.
  SUSANOO (his ULTIMATE, two-stage):
      Ultimate                    U            [ L2 / R2 ]   -> Stage 1 giant (costs ~50% energy)
      Ultimate again (after release)                         -> Stage 2 escalation.  Lasts ~13s.
      NOTE: while Susanoo is active the dōjutsu is disabled — Susanoo keeps the Special/Charge+Special buttons.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
SASUKE (ADULT)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Rinnegan suite (R1 / R2 / R3 / R5)  — works ANY time, no eye-set needed. Inputs as the shared list above.
  M1 Kagutsuchi:
      Cast Amaterasu              Up + Special         ( W + L )
      then steer with Up / Down, and re-press Up + Special to snuff it (+ refund).
  No Susanoo.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
SASUKE (SENSEI / Boruto-era — EYE-SET gated)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Cycle the active eye         Up + Ultimate   ( W + U )     Raiton -> Mangekyō -> Rinnegan -> (back to Raiton)
      (a small "SET" indicator shows the current eye. Plain Ultimate = his summon, NOT the cycle.)

  Rinnegan suite (R1 / R2 / R3 / R5)  — ONLY while the eye is on RINNEGAN
      i.e. press Up+Ultimate TWICE from the start, then use the shared Charge+Special inputs.
  M1 Kagutsuchi  — ONLY while the eye is on MANGEKYŌ (Up+Ultimate ONCE from the start):
      Cast Amaterasu              Forward + Special
      then steer with Up / Down, and re-press to snuff it (+ refund).
  No Susanoo.

========================================================================
WAR-SUSANO'O — TIER 1 (sasuke_sensei + sasuke_adult ONLY; teen Sasuke excluded)
How to actually trigger it + where to see the proof screenshots
========================================================================

★ IMPORTANT — WHY IT "DIDN'T WORK": the two Tier-1 moves are NOT on the
  normal purple special. They are:

  (1) SUSANO'O ARM GRAB   =  hold FORWARD + press GRAB (the "O" key)
         - NOT a Special. It's the GRAB button with Forward held.
         - Works in ANY eye-set (base form). A purple skeletal arm + claw
           shoots forward with long reach and grabs/throws. CHEAPEST move (15 chakra).
         - P1 keys: Forward = D (toward the foe) + Grab = O.

  (2) RIBCAGE GUARD       =  UP + SPECIAL, but ONLY while the SUSANO'O eye-set is active
         - You MUST first switch to the Susano'o eye-set:
              press  UP + ULTIMATE  (W + U)  to cycle the eye:
              Raiton -> Mangekyo -> Rinnegan -> SUSANO'O   (press it 3 times from the start;
              the active eye flashes above Sasuke's head).
         - THEN press  UP + SPECIAL  (W + L)  = the rib-ring guard shell (20 chakra).
         - TIP: tap Special quickly — if you HOLD Up too long first you'll JUMP
           instead (Up = jump). Hold Up and tap L almost together.

  Other Susano'o-set inputs (N/F/B/D Special + the Ultimate) are RESERVED —
  they do nothing yet (Tier 2 = torso, Tier 3 = Soldier; coming in later phases).

PROOF SCREENSHOTS (open these to see it working):
  harness/shots/SHOW_armgrab_reach.png   — the purple arm + claw reaching forward
  harness/shots/SHOW_armgrab_catch.png   — the arm catching the opponent
  harness/shots/SHOW_ribcage_guard.png   — the rib-cage guard shell wrapped around Sasuke
