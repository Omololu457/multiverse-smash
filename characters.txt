  - Damage / cost / stat numbers are pulled verbatim from characters.js
    `stats`, `basic_attacks`, `specials`, `transformations`, `ultimate`.

    multiplied by the GLOBAL_DAMAGE_SCALE (~0.60) — see per-char notes.
  - [S=yes] = hasSprites (real art wired).  [S=no] = box/procedural or dev stub.
  - "COMMAND / RUSH MOVES" = Fwd+Heavy rekka chains where present.
  - "POSSIBLE ADDITIONS" = ideas grounded in the sprite/asset comments already
    in the code (dormant frames, CUT moves, borrowed animations, missing
    win/lose/intro art). These are candidates for you to greenlight — NOT
    already-built content.
  - To change a move: find the character's object in characters.js by rosterKey
    and edit the matching field. Special/ultimate BEHAVIOUR lives in abilities.js.
