========================================================================
MULTIVERSE SMASH — MAPS AND CHARACTERS
========================================================================
Inventory from the current game files. Brackets contain the internal fighter
key so versions with similar names can be looked up in the code.

MAPS (35)
---------

- Jujutsu High Courtyard (Jujutsu Kaisen) — A hilltop school courtyard at dusk, with a tiled
    fighting floor, temple rooftops and spectators from Jujutsu High.

- Shibuya Incident (Jujutsu Kaisen) — A night market street under a cursed dome, with city
    towers, neon signs and escalating destruction in the background.

- Hidden Leaf Village (Naruto) — A sandy platform on a floating piece of Konoha, with village
    rooftops and the Hokage Monument behind it.

- Sunagakure (Naruto) — A sandstone plaza with domed homes, the Kazekage's building and sheer
    desert cliffs. A sandstorm brings Shukaku into view.

- Forest of Death (Naruto) — A fight under the roots of a giant tree, surrounded by dense
    forest, snakes and the Chūnin Exam tower in the distance.

- Valley of the End (Naruto) — A wet riverbank beneath the waterfall and the two colossal
    statues of Hashirama and Madara.

- Planet Namek (Dragon Ball) — Namek's alien terrain under Frieza's invasion, with his ship
    overhead, soldiers, explosions and fleeing villagers.

- World Tournament Arena (Dragon Ball) — The white-tiled outdoor tournament ring, with an
    entrance hall, banners, packed stands and an announcer.

- Infinity Castle (Demon Slayer) — A lantern-lit wooden platform inside the demons' sprawling
    castle, with rooms floating at different depths.

- Asakusa (Demon Slayer) — A Taishō-era Tokyo shopping street at night, with lanterns, shop
    signs, townspeople and the Ryōunkaku tower.

- Mugen Train (Demon Slayer) — A nighttime rail yard beside lit maroon carriages. Demons and
    Demon Slayers clash on the roofs and track bed.

- Citadel of Ricks (Rick and Morty) — A space-station hall with a portal-fluid reactor, opening
    portals and huge windows overlooking the Citadel and passing spacecraft.

- Bellwood (Ben 10) — A neighborhood main street with Mr. Smoothy, pigeons and onlookers. The
    fight draws a crowd, car alarms and police.

- Null Void (Ben 10) — A prison dimension full of drifting rock islands, glowing mist, scattered
    wreckage and a rift that pulls debris inward.

- Soul Society (Bleach) — A Seireitei courtyard framed by white walls, slate roofs and cherry
    blossoms, with Soul Reapers watching from the edges.

- Woodsboro (Scream / Horror) — A suburban street after dark. Phones ring, porch lights flicker,
    masked figures appear and police lights wash over the houses.

- Yorknew City (Hunter × Hunter) — The auction-week market district, with a tram, vendor stalls,
    rooftop Phantom Troupe figures and searchlights around the auction tower.

- Heaven's Arena (Hunter × Hunter) — A raised stone ring high above the clouds, with tall
    windows, tiered seats, banners and a scoreboard.

- Viltrumite Warzone (Invincible) — Scorched ruins beneath a red sky. Viltrumites fight overhead
    and a giant warrior statue overlooks the battlefield.

- Angel Grove (Power Rangers) — A wrecked downtown street where civilians flee, Rangers fight
    Krybots and Megazords battle giant monsters in the distance.

- Command Center (Power Rangers) — The open desert outside Angel Grove, with the Rangers'
    hilltop headquarters, teleport effects and Putty attacks in the background.

- PK Academy (The Disastrous Life of Saiki K.) — A bright schoolyard with cherry blossoms and
    students. A meteor appears late in the fight and Saiki stops it at the KO.

- Analysis Nexus (Original) — A dark teal data-grid arena with a luminous central core, orbiting
    rings and holographic readouts that react to the fight.

- The Originals (Original) — Rifts cycle between Omololu's multiverse lab, Dseyl's
    floating-island sky and Enoch's glacier-and-volcano landscape.

- Multiverse Roulette (Original) — A rift arena that starts with a randomly chosen stage
    backdrop and shifts to others during the match. Music comes from the stage playlists.

- Shadow Garden (Jujutsu Kaisen) — Megumi's Chimera Shadow Garden rendered as a pool of living
    shadow. Shikigami emerge during the fight and Mahoraga rises at the KO.

- Harbor Dock (Original arena) — A waterfront dock with barrels and bollards, moving water and a
    bobbing boat full of cheering spectators.

- Training Center (Original arena) — A bright 3D hologram room with a projection screen, a
    raised platform and a glowing cyan training ring.

- The Batcave (DC) — Batman's underground hangar, with a steel Bat-symbol deck, the Batwing, the
    Batcomputer and bats moving through the cavern.

- Krypton (DC) — Krypton's citadel on the planet's last day, with its dome torn open and an
    attack underway overhead.

- Arlong Park (One Piece) — Arlong's compound, with red gates, tangerine trees, fish-men,
    villagers and the sea cow. Arlong's tower topples at the KO.

- The Baratie (One Piece) — The deck of the floating restaurant, with cooks and spectators, a
    moored pirate ship, a sea battle and a Sea King offshore.

- Queens Alley (Marvel) — A New York alley at twilight with brownstones, fire escapes, graffiti
    and the Manhattan skyline. Spider-Man swings overhead.

- Stark Lab (Marvel) — An armor workshop with suits in glass pods, robot arms,
    holograms, a glowing reactor and a prototype aircraft.

- Battle Royale Colosseum (Free-for-all) — An extra-wide sand arena for free-for-all, with an
    iron gate, braziers, banners, a cheering crowd and fireworks.
