WHO HE IS
---------
Jack Landors is the Red Ranger of S.P.D. B-Squad (Power Rangers S.P.D.). He's a
former street thief who could walk through walls before he ever had a morpher.
- In game: "S.P.D. Red Ranger" (HUD: "S.P.D. Red"), in the Power Rangers universe.
- Archetype: Teleport Rushdown / Judge (primary rushdown, secondary zoner).
- Difficulty: 4. Tagline: "S.P.D. Emergency!"
- He is always morphed: the sprite sheet has no unmorphed Jack.

The game plan in one sentence: blink in on teleport charges, hit from behind
with crossups, build Delta, and at the end pass JUDGMENT on a weakened foe to
seal them in a containment card for the finish.


CONTROLS (P1 keyboard defaults; all rebindable)
-----------------------------------------------
  Move ............ A / D  (W = jump/up, S = down)
  Light (A) ....... J
  Heavy (B) ....... K
  Up-Attack ....... I
  Special ......... L
  Ultimate ........ U
  Grab ............ O
  Block ........... ;
The code calls Light "A" and Heavy "B" because the sprite sheet does.
Motion inputs are facing-relative: "→" means toward your opponent.


THE STAT LINE
-------------
  Health ...... 1200 in characters data (the kit text and BALANCE_NOTES still say
                950; it was raised later in the roster-parity HP pass)
  Energy ...... "Delta", max 100 (cyan bar)
  Walk ........ 5.6 px per frame
  Run ......... 8.4 px per frame (hold the direction after a ground blink)
  Jumps ....... 1 (no double jump, no flight)
  Weight ...... 0.9 (light: he takes a bit more knockback)
  Armour ...... none.  Reflect: none.
The engine's normal double-tap dash is OFF for him. His double-tap is the
BLINK teleport instead (next section).


1. BLINK: HIS TELEPORT (the heart of the character)
---------------------------------------------------
Input: double-tap ← or → (works on the ground AND in the air).
- Distance: 22% of the screen.
- Length: 11 frames = 3 startup, then 5 INVULNERABLE travel frames, then 3 recovery.
- Charges: 2. Each refills over 90 frames (1.5 seconds).
- Cost: NO Delta. It only spends a charge.
- No vertical blink: you can't teleport straight up.

PHASE STEP (crossups):
- Blinking THROUGH the opponent costs no extra charge.
- It leaves the opponent facing the WRONG way for 8 frames, which breaks
  forward-committed attacks and makes your next hit come from behind.

DODGE REWARD:
- If your blink's travel passes through a LIVE enemy hitbox or projectile, you
  get +5 Delta.
- Your next attack started within 20 frames does +15% damage. A white flash on
  the streak tells you it worked.

BLINK-CANCELS (each costs one charge):
- Delta Charge: double-tap again when it hits OR is blocked.
- Sky Uppercut: double-tap during its airborne recovery.
While a blink is happening he can't attack; the blink owns him for 11 frames.


2. DELTA: HIS METER
-------------------
How he gains Delta:
  +4 ... every hit that lands (melee or a Blaster bolt)
  +2 ... every hit the foe blocks
  +1 ... every 40 frames passively (about 1.5 per second)
  +5 ... every blink dodge
  +40 .. an INNOCENT Judgment verdict
Specials spend their Delta cost the moment they start. A Special press you
can't afford is simply eaten; there is no fallback move.
After the Super, Delta is LOCKED for 3 seconds: no gain at all.


3. NORMALS (all blunt damage)
-----------------------------
On-block numbers are frame advantage: + means you act first, − means the foe does.

  Jab ............ Light .................. 40 dmg, 5f startup, +1 on block
                   (his safest button: you keep your turn even if they block)
  Straight ....... Light, Light  (or →+Light) .. 60 dmg, 8f, −3 on block
  Kick ........... Heavy  (or Light ×3) ...... 65 dmg, 9f, −3 on block, long reach
  Heavy kick ..... →+Heavy ................ 85 dmg, 12f, −5 on block. LAUNCHES.
                   Hold ↑ as it connects = LAUNCHER JUMP-CANCEL: the recovery is
                   skipped and he jumps up after them for an air kick.
  Low kick ....... ↓+Light ................ 45 dmg, 7f, hits LOW (must be crouch-blocked)
  Rising kick .... ↑+Heavy or Up-Attack ... 80 dmg, 9f, tall anti-air hitbox, launches
  Air kick ....... Light/Heavy in the air .. 65 dmg, 4f, fast, tall hitbox
                   (meets a launched foe on the way up)
  Flip kick ...... ↓ + attack in the air ... 60 dmg, spikes downward
  Acrobatic flip . ←+Heavy ................ back-handspring escape: 112 px backward,
                                            invulnerable for 8 frames, no hitbox
  Wake-up reversal ↓+Heavy, or ANY attack as he stands up after a knockdown:
                   70 dmg, 6 invulnerable frames, but −12 on block (very punishable)
  Grab / Throw ... O ...................... 35 dmg (the shared grab)

CHAINS (gatlings: cancel a normal into the next on hit OR block):
  Jab ........ → Straight, Low kick, Kick, Heavy kick, Rising kick
  Low kick ... → Straight, Heavy kick, Rising kick
  Straight ... → Kick, Heavy kick, Rising kick
  Kick ....... → Heavy kick, Rising kick
Any connected normal can also cancel into ANY special or the Super.
Button presses are buffered for 8 frames (on the sim clock), so a press during
hit-freeze still comes out.


4. SPECIALS (cost = Delta)
--------------------------
  DELTA BLASTER .... ↓+Special (or plain Special; works in the air) ... 10 Delta
     A full-screen blaster bolt, 50 dmg (projectile). At most 4 of his bolts on
     screen at once. 10f startup on the ground, 8f in the air.

  RAPID FIRE ....... keep ↓+Special HELD through the Blaster wind-up .... +10 (20 total)
     Five tracer bolts × 25 dmg at 10 per second, with a slight spread (decided
     by a hash, never random, so replays and LAN match).

  DELTA CHARGE ..... →→ + Special .............................. 15 Delta
     A handstand dash kick across 40% of the screen, 95 dmg, KNOCKDOWN.
     Blink-cancel it on hit or block. If your →→ already started a blink, a
     quick Special refunds the charge and turns it into Delta Charge instead.

  PHASE STRIKE ..... ↓↘→ + Special (ground) .................... 18 Delta
     He phases THROUGH the foe on frame 3 (always crosses up; reach 45% of the
     screen) and lands a flying kick from behind, 100 dmg. Invulnerable on
     frames 0-9, so it passes through attacks. +8 on hit, so a Jab links after.

  SKY UPPERCUT ..... ↑ + Special ............................... 20 Delta
     A rising split kick, 110 dmg, 6f startup, launches; he rises with it.
     Blink out of the airborne recovery to stay safe. His main anti-air answer.

  DELTAMAX STRIKE .. ↓↙← + Special (ground) .................... 25 Delta
     He braces, then fires a beam across 70% of the screen: 120 dmg (energy).
     It ERASES enemy projectiles it touches. 18f startup, his slowest special,
     but it still links off the Straight's 22f hitstun.

  JUDGMENT ......... Light + Heavy + Special together (meter full) .. free
     See section 5.


5. JUDGMENT: THE SIGNATURE MECHANIC
-----------------------------------
The Judgment meter (separate from Delta):
- Every hit you land is +1 point; it fills at 20.
- Once full, press Light + Heavy + Special within 4 ticks of each other.
- The press can cancel a just-started normal or a connected one. If Special
  started a move first, its Delta is refunded.

What happens:
- Jack points the morpher, time FREEZES for 30 frames (0.5 s) while it scans,
  and he is invulnerable.
- The verdict depends on the foe's HP:

  GUILTY (foe at or below 30% HP):
    - The foe is sealed in the gem containment card for 3 seconds and CANNOT act.
    - The card absorbs 50% of every hit you land on them.
    - When it ends, they're released at the FAR side of the screen.
    - Your Super beam this round becomes 220 instead of 180.
  INNOCENT (foe above 30%):
    - You get +40 Delta.
    - The meter stays full for 5 seconds, so you get ONE re-attempt.

Limits:
- Once per round.
- A Ranger opponent is ALWAYS innocent (Rangers can't be judged).


6. SUPER: "S.P.D. EMERGENCY: DELTA SQUAD FULL POWER"
----------------------------------------------------
Input: ↓↘→↓↘→ + Special, or the Ultimate button (U). Needs 100 Delta (all of it).
1. A WANTED-poster flash.
2. Three blink strikes from three angles (front, behind, below), 55 dmg each.
3. Then the Deltamax beam: 180 dmg, or 220 if you got a GUILTY verdict this round.
- Total: 345 (385 after GUILTY).
- He's invulnerable for the whole scripted sequence (110 frames).
- Afterwards: all Delta is spent, Delta is locked for 3 s, and BOTH teleport
  charges refill instantly.


7. COMBO SCALING (why long combos don't do 4× damage)
-----------------------------------------------------
- Each extra hit in a combo does 85% of the previous scale: hit 1 = 100%,
  hit 2 = 85%, hit 3 = 72%, hit 4 = 61%.
- The combo counter resets after 1.5 s with no hit.
- On-screen damage = raw × 0.6 (the global damage scale every fighter has).
- Super hits keep the scale from the moment the Super started.


8. THE FIVE MAIN COMBOS (measured with real input; "dmg" = what the HP bar shows)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1) CHARGE OUT, meterless-ish (15 Delta), 117 dmg
   Light, Light, Light, →→+Special (Delta Charge), then →→ on hit
   = Jab, Straight, Kick, Delta Charge, then a blink to safety behind the
     knocked-down foe or back out. The four hits pay back +16 Delta.

2) SKY ROUTE (20 Delta), 138 dmg
   ↓+Light, →+Heavy (hold ↑ as it hits), Light in the air, ↑+Special
   = Low kick, launcher, jump-cancel, air kick, Sky Uppercut. Blink out after.

3) PHASE BEAM (43 Delta), 150 dmg (his best normal combo)
   ↓↘→+Special, Light, Light, ↓↙←+Special
   = Phase Strike crossup, Jab, Straight, Deltamax beam.

4) DODGE PUNISH (30 Delta), 126 dmg
   ↓+Special at range, then →→ THROUGH their answer (so the invulnerable travel
   covers their attack), →+Heavy within 20 frames (+15%), then ↑+Special.
   Not a true combo (they act between hits 1 and 2), but the counter carries.

5) THE SENTENCE (100 Delta + GUILTY), about 284 dmg
   Chip them below 30% with a full Judgment meter, Light+Heavy+Special (GUILTY),
   Light, Light, ↓↙←+Special on the sealed foe, then after release
   ↓↘→↓↘→+Special for the 220-beam Super.
   In a real match at under 30% HP, this kills well before the end.


9. HOW TO PLAY HIM
------------------
- Neutral: poke with Delta Blaster bolts (cheap, full-screen, 4 max) and wait
  for them to commit. Then blink THROUGH the attack for the dodge reward and
  punish with a +15% Heavy kick.
- Pressure: the Jab is +1 on block, so Jab, then blink crossup (they face the
  wrong way for 8f), then Jab again is his loop. Keep both charges in mind:
  with both spent you have no escape for 1.5 s, and that's when they hit back.
- Anti-air: Rising kick (cheap), Sky Uppercut (20 Delta, launches), or the air
  Blaster. He has one jump and no vertical blink, so flyers are his hardest
  matchup.
- Defense: Acrobatic flip (8 invulnerable frames), Phase Strike (invulnerable
  0-9) passes through attacks, and the Wake-up reversal is a get-off-me button
  but is −12 if blocked.
- Endgame: count your Judgment points. Don't waste the verdict early: INNOCENT
  only pays 40 Delta, while GUILTY at 30% HP basically ends the round.
- Meter discipline: Super = ALL your Delta plus a 3 s lockout. Use it to finish,
  not to start.
