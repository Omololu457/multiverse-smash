# Beast Boy — canon adaptation

Canon reference: https://www.dc.com/characters/beast-boy — Garfield "Gar" Logan, Teen Titans; green-skinned shapeshifter who becomes any animal. Art: the supplied Game Boy Advance *Teen Titans* playable-character sheet (Mr. C rip; credit panel quoted in credits.js).

## What is canon / from the sheet
- Identity, green skin, purple-and-grey suit, pointed ears and fang (sheet pixels, unchanged).
- The four animals and every action are exactly the sheet's sequences: Gorilla Kick, Changing into Triceratops (reverse to change back) + Triceratops Charging, Changing into Bird + Bird flight, Tiger Slash, Punch 1 / 2, Aerial Kick, Appearing, Idle, Running, Jumping, Landing, Hurt, Defeated, health-icon expressions and the cut-in bust + mouths. No fifth animal and no invented frames.

## Game adaptations (not canon mechanics)
- **One strike per animal**: each animal is a SHORT committed attack — transform → one hit opportunity → recover → humanoid. No controllable animal fighter, no permanent animal mode, no transformation selector, no free bird flight, no invented animal moveset. Bird Intercept is a bounded authored arc (≤ 24 active f) with a forced return.
- **Wild Chain** (Gorilla → Bird → Tiger → Triceratops → Gorilla): a 10 f window after a CLEAN animal hit; fresh matching direction + Special; executes at the authored return boundary; full cost; max 2 links; no repeats; never on block / whiff / parry / armor.
- **Instinct**: three distinct animal hits in a round → one token; consumed when the next animal starts; lets that move's last 8 f cancel into an ordinary jump or dash. No damage, no extra link.
- **Morph Energy** (100): the shared energy bar renamed; costs per animal; L2 / Charge is the ordinary shared charge.
- **ANIMAL INSTINCT — FOUR BEAST FINISH** ultimate: a humanoid punch opener then the four supplied animal sequences in a frozen cinematic (canon-flavoured, not a canon scene).
- **Voice**: no voice pack supplied → no dialogue, no synthesised speech. SFX are deterministic WebAudio placeholders (morph, gorilla, triceratops, bird, tiger, chain ready, instinct).
- **Skins**: ten cosmetic palettes; none is a canonical Beast Boy outfit (crossover palettes are colour schemes only; see docs/skins/beast_boy/FORM_PALETTE_MAP.md).
