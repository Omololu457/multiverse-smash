# Ben 10 cast — voice-over map

Generated by `tools/ben10/write_audio_map.py` from `docs/ben10/AUDIO_ANALYSIS.json` (`tools/ben10/classify_vo.py`). Every clip was sorted by **waveform shape only** (ffprobe duration; peak / RMS; zero-crossing rate; spectral centroid; voiced-energy ratio; silence ratio; loudness slope). **Nobody listened to these clips and no clip was transcribed**, so every pool assignment is a shape guess for a human to spot-check.

## Phase 0 findings

- **Vilgax**: a roster character (`characters.vilgax`, universe `ben_10`, sprite-backed). He already had a thin, transcript-verified pack (`vilgaxVoice.js`: 1 taunt + 2 grunts). The owner chose to **copy and wire** the 23 new clips (`assets/audio/vilgax/source/`). His old clips are merged into the matching pools, and his voice now uses the same deterministic dispatch.
- **Kevin 11**: no Kevin 11 character exists on committed `main`. (A separate `kevin_levin` character is being built in another session's uncommitted work.) The 41 `kevin11` clips are copied to `assets/audio/_unassigned/kevin11/` and are **not wired**, per the brief. They can be routed to that character once it lands.
- **READY twins**: every clip goes to the READY alien key, never the legacy experimental twin: diamondhead → `diamondhead_ov`, feedback → `feedback_ov`, upgrade → `upgrade_ov`. The legacy twins have no pools and stay silent.
- **No READY alien had VO before**: only Ben 10's procedural WebAudio synth (`sound.playBen10`). That synth is unchanged.

## Mechanism (reused, not new)

`ben10VoiceMap.js` copies the `homelander_sfx_map.js` dispatch:
- The clip is `juicePickVariant(hash(match seed, frame), player)`.
- Pitch is ±3% for voice and ±5% for SFX (`juicePitch`).
- Per-event cooldowns: hit 30f, attack 45f, special 24f, taunt 90f; intro, win, lose and transform always play.
- Voice uses `playSfxFile({ owner, fadeMs: 40 })`: one voice per character, a 40 ms crossfade, and music ducking.
- No random source. Cooldown stamps live in a module WeakMap, never in GAME_STATE.

**Triggers:**
- intro: the match intro beat; the select-screen bark is a deterministic pick.
- win / lose: the victory block. A transformed fighter speaks as the alien, then falls back to the human pool.
- transform: the Omnitrix transform / swap / Vow-pick cue plays SPECIAL_CALLOUT (else TAUNT) plus a layered SFX.
- special: a form special's synth cue, or a special / ultimate connect.
- attack: a clean connect. taunt: a long-string connect. hit: taking a hit.
- lowHealth: Albedo only, using his transcript-verified line.

**Rules:**
- A clip over 8 s is EXCLUDED as "unverified, possible music".
- Near-silent clips are excluded.
- Clips from an UNVERIFIED-era folder can land only in ATTACK_EFFORT / SFX / TAUNT.
- INTRO / WIN / LOSE from verified folders were assigned **by shape** (owner's choice) and are flagged *wording unverified*.
