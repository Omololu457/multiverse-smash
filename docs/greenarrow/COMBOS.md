# Green Arrow — Bread-and-Butter Combos

Scaling: **85% per hit** (cumulative); the combo counter **resets after 1.5 s idle**. Damage
below is approximate (varies with scaling, tags and drone synergies). All five work in training.

Move damages (pre-scale): Jab 35 · Straight 65 · Elbow 65 · High kick 60 · Spinning back kick 85
(launcher) · Crouch jab 30 · Crouch kick 45 · Low arrow 40 · Slide 50 (knockdown) · Rising kick 70 ·
Bow slash (→+H) 75 · Air shot 40 · Plain shot 45 (charged 70 + pierce) · Anti-air shot 45 · Fire
arrow 60 (+burn) · Ice arrow 40 (freeze) · Explosive arrow 80 · EMP 25 · Trick shot 3×30.

---

### 1. Pressure reset — `Jab → Straight → Bow slash → Kestrel (→+Special)`
`35 + 55 + 54` ≈ **144**, then Kestrel strafes to cover your reset. Ends plus; the gunship's
chip keeps the opponent pinned while you re-establish spacing.

### 2. OTG punish — `Crouch kick → Slide → Explosive arrow (↓↓+H)`
`45 + 43` (slide knocks down) `+ 68` (explosive hits **OTG** on the downed foe) ≈ **156**.
Slide's hard knockdown sets up the explosive arrow's over-the-ground detonation.

### 3. Juggle — `Spinning back kick (launch) → Air shot → land → Anti-air shot`
`85 + 34` (air shot mid-juggle) `+ 33` (anti-air catches the falling foe) ≈ **152**. The spinning
back kick launches; chase with the diagonal air shot, land, and the 60° anti-air shot re-catches.

### 4. Ice-Hook carry — `Ice arrow → dash → Elbow → Spinning back kick → Skyhook (Ice Hook)`
`40` (freeze 0.7 s) → dash in → `55 + 61` → with a Skyhook out, the frozen state triggers **Ice
Hook** (instant hook from anywhere) → `90` drop. ≈ **246** into a corner carry. Needs a Skyhook
deployed (↑+Special) beforehand.

### 5. Tagged Ignition — `(Tagged) Charged plain shot through Aegis → Fire arrow → Kestrel FIRE2 "Ignition"`
With the opponent **Overwatch-tagged** and an **Aegis** between you: charged plain shot = `70
×1.15 (Aegis "charged") ×1.10 (tagged)` ≈ `88 (+pierce)`. Then a **Fire arrow** while they're under
**Kestrel FIRE2** → **Ignition** `+30` burst = `(60+30)×0.85` ≈ `76`, plus burn and the stream's
chip. ≈ **164 + DoT**. The flagship zoner sequence — four systems (tag, shield-charge, ignition,
suppression) stacking at once.

---

**Drone synergies recap:** arrows through **Aegis** → +15% "charged" (cyan trail); **Fire arrow** on
a foe under **Kestrel FIRE2** → **Ignition** +30; **Ice-frozen** foe + **Skyhook** → instant hook;
**Explosive arrow** near a **Bulwark** → 3-missile salvo; an **Overwatch-tagged** foe can't tech
the Skyhook drop and eats +10% from every arrow (which also home slightly).

## PASS 2 combos (Net + Kamikaze)

### 6. Net lockdown → Kamikaze — `Kestrel (Strike, →+Heavy+Special) → Net arrow (quiver) → Kamikaze connects`
Deploy a **Strike Kestrel** (Kamikaze). Fire a **Net arrow** (quiver: ↓↙←+Up) — the foe is netted
1.0s (and grounded 1.2s if they were flying). The Kamikaze dives into the pinned target and gets the
**+25% netted bonus**: `90 × 1.25` ≈ **112** on top of the net lockdown. The read that deletes a flyer.

### 7. Net → Ice-Hook-style carry — `Net arrow → Skyhook (Strike, ↑+Heavy+Special) hooks from anywhere → charged plain shot`
With a Skyhook out, landing a **Net arrow** triggers the **net-hook synergy** — the Skyhook hooks the
netted foe **from anywhere on screen** (like Ice-Hook), carries them to the corner and drops for 90.
Follow the hard knockdown with a **charged plain shot** (hold Light, ≥40f → 70 + pierce) ≈ **160+** and
a corner reset. Net's 6s cooldown gates the loop.
