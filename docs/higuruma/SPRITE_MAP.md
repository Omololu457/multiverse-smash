## Uncertainties and limits (art-honest)

- **b02_0–1** read as a crouched step; used for walking back. Their original purpose is not labelled on the sheet.
- **Thrown / recalled gavel (optional §8): NOT implemented.** b14_3 shows the gavel on a wavy cord that is still in his hand, i.e. a tethered extension,
  not a detached projectile; there is no return / catch art. It is used as the Forward + Heavy Extended Lash, and L2 keeps the shared charge.
- **J3** art is a spinning LOW sweep (b11_6–11), not a short finishing swing; it is used as the chain finisher, and the deviation is documented in MOVESET.md.
- **Air normal** art (b10_5–6) is an upward swing, so its box sits above-forward rather than below.
- Swing arcs merge into the gavel caps with no outline. Skin recolours separate metal from effect pixels by distance to the gavel wood (3 / 7 px); a few edge
  pixels of an arc can take the metal colour (see SKINS.md).
- There is no Judgeman, courtroom or opponent art on the sheet. The Deadly Sentencing courtroom is simple code-drawn rails, bench and floor marks, and nothing more is implied.
- The sword cells (b14_5–9) are the only Executioner's Sword art; the sword is never generated or redrawn.
