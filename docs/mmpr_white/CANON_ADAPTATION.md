# White Ranger — canon notes and game adaptation

**Canon (general franchise knowledge, Mighty Morphin Power Rangers seasons 2-3 / *The Movie*):** Tommy Oliver, karate champion and former Green Ranger, returns as the White Ranger and team leader. Weapon: Saba, the talking sabre. Thunderzord: White Tigerzord; in *The Movie* era the team pilots Ninja Zords that combine into the Ninja Megazord (Tommy's own Ninjazord is the Falcon).

**Supplied art:** Sega Genesis *Mighty Morphin Power Rangers: The Movie* sheets, White Ranger (460×996) + Ninja Megazord (410×787), "Sprites ripped by Belial the Hedgehog" / "Sprites ripped by Belial". Both GIFs preserved unmodified; hashes in START_STATE.md / source_rects.json. Tigerzord, Thunder Megazord and Falconzord sheets were not used.

## Canon-faithful
- A precise martial artist: open-hand strikes (HAND), kicks (FOOT) and Saba cuts (BLADE), built only from the sheet's own frames.
- Saba appears only where the sheet draws it (one blade cell + the slash streak + chest-grip cells). No sword was added to other poses.
- "White Tiger" names (Rising Tiger, Tiger Heel, White Tiger Ascent, Tiger Descent) nod to the White Tigerzord without using its art.
- Leader identity: the team's Ninja Megazord answers Tommy's orders.

## Explicit GAME ADAPTATIONS
- **Ninja Megazord as a called assist.** It is the TEAM's combined Megazord, presented as team support answering the leader, never as "Tommy's Zord" in any text. It acts automatically after the call; the player controls only Tommy.
- **Discipline Formation** (HAND + FOOT + BLADE → one token that upgrades one order) is a game mechanic expressing "a leader who earns team conversion by technique". It is deliberately not a mark-and-call system (Black's Pincer tags the foe; White marks nothing on the opponent).
- **Saba's Answer feint**, Leader's Return (blink from the call pose) and the shared Ranger teleport are game mechanics, not canon powers.
- **White Light — Ninja Grand Formation** (ultimate) is an original combined beat sequence, not a scene from the show.
- **Six inspired skins** are colour interpretations borrowed from Ben 10 aliens on the unchanged White Ranger pixels (each entry carries its source and caveat). The Saba blade, outline, visor and the shared robot are never recoloured.
- **Sound** is synthesized SFX only; no voice lines (no fake Tommy voice).
