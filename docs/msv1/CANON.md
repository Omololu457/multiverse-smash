# MSV1 — Power Rangers Ninja Storm: canon notes

Source of truth for names, lines and what may or may not be drawn in game.

## Setting
- **Wind Ninja Academy**: the hidden ninja school where Shane, Tori and Dustin
  train. Its students were the only ones left after Lothor attacked, and Sensei
  Watanabe made them Wind Rangers.
- **Lothor**: the exiled ninja who is the series villain. His monsters attack
  the city each episode.

## Equipment
- **Wind Morpher**: the wrist morpher. Morph call: **"Ninja Storm, Ranger Form! Ha!"**
  It can also fire ninja energy beams (used in game as the Ninja Beam special).
- **Ninja Sword**: each Wind Ranger's sword. Sword and sheath combine into a
  **Laser Blaster**, which is the in-air diagonal beam.
- **Ninja Streak**: the Rangers' high-speed run, shown as a coloured speed
  smear. This is the sheet's R3 blur row and the core movement option in game.

## The rangers
| | Shane Clarke | Dustin Brooks |
|---|---|---|
| Colour | Red Wind Ranger | **Yellow** Wind Ranger |
| Element | Air | Earth |
| Role | Team leader | Teammate |
| Hobby | Skateboarding | Motocross |
| Personal | Hawk Blaster, Hawkzord, later the Battlizer | Lion Hammer, Lionzord |
| Personality | Serious, driven leader | Kind, upbeat, a little gullible ("Dude!") |

Both ride the **Storm Chargers** (team motorbikes). Dustin is the motocross rider.

**Canon guard:** the Yellow Wind Ranger is **Dustin** (Earth). **Tori** is the
**Blue** Wind Ranger (Water). The yellow ranger is never called Tori anywhere
in this build.

## Why the illusion mode exists
**Copybot**, a Lothor monster, created shadow copies of the Rangers. Ninja
Mirror (one real body, one illusion) and the two shadow clones are based on that.

## Not drawn in game
Neither sprite sheet contains the **Hawk Blaster**, **Lion Hammer**, the
**Battlizer**, any Zord or the Storm Chargers. **They are NOT drawn in game.**
No art is invented for them. They are named only in bios and flavour text.
Moves use only sheet art: sword, crescent arcs, Morpher beam, kunai, drill
tornado, electric dome and coloured smoke.
