# MSV1-GOJO — Canon notes (Satoru Gojo, Shinjuku Showdown arc)

Source: Jujutsu Kaisen manga, Shinjuku Showdown (ch. 222–236). These notes say what each mechanic is modelled on and
where the game adapts it.

## Limitless (無下限呪術)
Gojo's inherited technique brings Zeno's "convergent infinite series" into reality: space between him and something
else can be made infinitely divisible, so the thing never arrives.

* **Infinity (無下限)** is the neutral state of Limitless. Anything approaching slows down as it gets closer and never
  touches him. It is automatic (the Six Eyes sort what is let through) and costs very little because the Six Eyes run
  it efficiently. **Game:** P toggles it, it is invisible (canon: you cannot see it, you only find you cannot reach him),
  and there are three intensities. The only sign it is on is the CE bar draining.
* **Lapse: Blue (術式順転「蒼」)** is the "positive" amplification of Limitless: it creates an attraction point that
  pulls everything in and crushes it. Gojo also uses it to move himself (the "teleport" and flight: he pulls himself
  toward a point), and he boosts punches with it (Blue-enhanced strikes against Sukuna). **Maximum Output: Blue** is a
  large, slow, dragging orb (vs Sukuna in Shibuya and Shinjuku). **Game:** Lapse Blue, Maximum Output Blue, Blue Step,
  Blue Fist.
* **Reversal: Red (術式反転「赫」)** is the reversed technique, fed by Reverse Cursed Technique energy: a repulsion
  burst. In Shinjuku he plants a **delayed Red** and detonates it later, and fires it point-blank from the fingertip.
  **Game:** Reversal Red (reflects projectiles), Delayed Red, Red Rush.
* **Hollow Technique: Purple (虚式「茈」)** merges Blue and Red into an "imaginary mass" that erases what it passes
  through. In Shinjuku he fires **200% Hollow Purple** after the full **incantation**, which raises its output
  (incantations are a binding-vow style power boost). **Game:** Hollow Purple erases every non-fighter object in its
  path; 200% Purple is a timing-press chant that climbs 100% → 200%.

### The 200% incantation (as printed on the sheet's chant strips — see LEDGER.md for the exact transcription)
Canon lines: *"Phase. Twilight. Eyes of Wisdom."* (位相 黄昏 智慧の瞳) — in the sheet's romaji the strips read the
Japanese words, then the final name **"Kyoshiki Murasaki"** (虚式「茈」). The lines refer to Nine Ropes, Polarized Light,
Crow and Declaration, Between Front and Back (九綱・偏光・烏と声明・表裏の間), which is the incantation he uses for the
200% shot. The game captions use the words exactly as they appear on the sheet.

## Six Eyes (六眼)
Lets him perceive cursed energy at atomic precision: it makes Limitless usable at all, makes Infinity cheap, and lets
him see through disguises and hidden presences. **Game:** Perfect Output (a frame-perfect cancel refunds CE),
Sight (clones/illusions/burrowing/stealth do not fool him), Efficiency (why Level 1 Infinity is cheap).

## Reverse Cursed Technique (反転術式)
Multiplying negative energy to create positive energy: he heals his body and, crucially in Shinjuku, **repairs his
brain** to recover from technique burnout after domains. **Game:** RCT converts CE into HP; held in burnout it
performs the Brain Reset and ends burnout early.

## Shinjuku specifics used by the game
* **Domain clashes with Malevolent Shrine.** Unlimited Void vs Malevolent Shrine collide repeatedly; Gojo uses a
  barrier-less tactic and a 0.2 s domain. Both suffer burnout afterwards. **Game:** domain clash mash-off; both burn out.
* **Technique burnout and brain repair.** After every domain his technique is burnt out; he uses RCT on his brain
  to recover. **Game:** 5 s burnout after Unlimited Void (Infinity off, no Blue/Red/Purple/Blue Step), nosebleed,
  RCT Brain Reset.
* **Black Flashes on Sukuna.** He lands several Black Flashes (cursed-energy impact within 0.000001 s of the hit),
  entering "the zone". **Game:** a 2-frame timing window on B / C / Blue Fist → ×2.5, +10 CE, THE ZONE.
* **Falling Blossom Emotion (落花の情)** — a Zen'in/Gojo anti-domain technique: cursed energy wraps the body and
  counters automatically on contact. Used against the sure-hit. **Game:** an auto-counter that works even when
  Infinity cannot.
* **Blue-boosted punches, delayed Red, Maximum Output Blue, 200% Purple** — all present in the fight.
* **Mahoraga adapting to Infinity.** Sukuna has Mahoraga adapt to Infinity; every adaptation turns its wheel, and once
  adapted it ignores Infinity. **Game:** the Handler's Mahoraga form: 3 wheel turns from stopped hits → ignores Infinity.
* **World-cutting slash.** Sukuna learns, via Mahoraga, a Dismantle that cuts space itself and ignores Infinity. **Game:**
  Sukuna has no such move in this roster, so the bypass is not implemented (reported).
