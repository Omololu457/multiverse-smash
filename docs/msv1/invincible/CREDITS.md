# MSV1-INVINCIBLE — credits

**Invincible sprites by JustOruma (SWL+)** — the "Invincible" SWL+ sprite sheet (DeviantArt / Discord / YouTube: JustOruma;
"SWL+ by Oruma"), supplied by the owner and kept unmodified at `assets/incoming/invincible/invincible.png`.

* In game: `credits.js` entry `invincible` (shown on the Credits screen) — "Invincible sprites by JustOruma (SWL+)".
* Every gameplay frame, effect and the face portrait are JustOruma's pixels, keyed and re-packed nearest-neighbour by
  `tools/invincible/slice.py`; the 20 premade skins are recolours of those same pixels (`tools/invincibleskins/`).
* Not used from the sheet: the header banner, the credit boxes / SWL+ logo / JUST ORUMA wordmark, the costume sample strip
  and the large standing figure (LEDGER.md).
* Invincible / Mark Grayson / Omni-Man are characters of the *Invincible* comic (Robert Kirkman, Cory Walker, Ryan
  Ottley; Image Comics / Skybound) and the Amazon animated series. This is a non-commercial fan tribute.
