# Ninja Storm Rangers — Research notes (Step C)

**Shane Clarke (Red Wind Ranger).** Wind Ninja Academy student, Air ninja, skater and the team's leader. Arsenal: twin
Ninja Swords and the Hawk Blaster. Zord: Hawk Zord. Gets his own Lightning Mode upgrade.
→ Kit: Air-flavoured rushdown. Crescent Blade / Crescent Wave are the swords, Hawk Blaster Lock-On is the blaster,
LIGHTNING MODE is the timed form, THUNDER FIST FINISH is the ultimate.

**Dustin Brooks (Yellow Wind Ranger).** Wind Ninja Academy student, Earth ninja, motocross rider, the team's laid-back
physical powerhouse. Arsenal: Lion Hammer. Zord: Lion Zord. Gets his own Lightning upgrade.
→ Kit: the same slots with Earth names (Tackle / Stomp Kick / Rising Slam; Earth Blade / Boulder Wave / Sand Spin
Throw / Lion Hammer Lock-On). QUAKE MODE is his timed form, LION ZORD SLAM is his ultimate. He is heavier (weight 0.85 vs 0.8).

**Shadow Clone / Kawarimi.** These are an **original**, ninjutsu-flavoured mechanic built for this game. The show never
shows literal shadow clones or a substitution jutsu. `BALANCE_NOTES.md` says so too.

**Ninja Vanish.** Ninja Storm Rangers teleport in a burst of wind / smoke. The game uses S.P.D. Red's existing Blink
numbers (rangers.js) under that name, so the two Rangers share one teleport ruleset.
