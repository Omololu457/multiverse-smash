# Sanji — Credits

* **Character:** Sanji — *One Piece* (Eiichiro Oda / Shueisha / Toei Animation).
* **Sprites:** Game Boy Advance *One Piece* sprite sheet, **ripped by Paddy**.
  The sheet carries the visible note "Ripped by Paddy / No credit needed". Credit is given here anyway.
* **Excluded, credit:** the text panels "Ripped by Paddy / No credit needed" (bottom right of R9) are never copied
  into the game. They are the only pixels on the sheet that are not used.
* **Going Merry figurehead:** the game already ships the One Piece emblem (`assets/zoro/merry.png`, added with Roronoa
  Zoro from the same ripper). Sanji reuses it; the sheet's copy is not duplicated.
* Original pixels are kept 1:1 (nearest-neighbour, mirrored to face right). Derived copies (mirror, crop, the
  Diable Jambe orange tint of the blue kick arcs) are listed in `docs/sanji/SPRITE_LEDGER.md`.
* **Sound:** WebAudio synth placeholders (no audio assets).
