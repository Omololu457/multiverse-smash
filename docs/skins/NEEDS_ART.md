# Appearances requiring art

## Krybot

- **Blue-Head Krybot:** distinct helmet, blue shoulder armor, belt/cuffs and weapon construction. Needs its own complete action/reaction sheets, portrait, pivots and verified model reference. Ordinary silver-head pixels cannot become this model by recoloring. No selectable skin ID registered. [Production costume reference](https://entertainment.ha.com/itm/movie-tv-memorabilia/costumes/power-rangers-spd-bvs-entertainment-2005-pair-of-2-blue-head-krybot-costumes-and-2-accessori/a/7354-11281.s).
- **Orange-Head Krybot:** pyramid head, separate armor silhouette and arm blades. Needs complete model-specific body/weapon art and portrait, including airborne, damage and grounded angles. No selectable skin ID registered. [Production components reference](https://entertainment.ha.com/itm/movie-tv-memorabilia/costumes/power-rangers-spd-bvs-entertainment-2005-group-of-11-orange-head-krybot-suit-components-and-accessories/a/7354-11273.s).
- **Exact Super Legends DS costume/weapon recreation:** implemented DS skins transfer palettes only. Exact DS shoulder/trim geometry, proportions and wrist blade require new compatible body/weapon art; no DS frames are borrowed. This is an accuracy limit of the two implemented palette adaptations, not an additional empty skin.

All new model sets would need idle, locomotion, jab/strike/kick, guard, jump/air/tumble, aiming, hurt, knockdown/recovery, helper and cinematic poses compatible with the existing fighter. Art alone would not authorize new model-exclusive gameplay.

Unverified GBA/PS2/PC palette variants and invented stealth/gold/corrupted/ghost/Ranger finishes have no skin entries. Obtain a documented distinct official appearance before treating these as art requirements. See [Krybot source decisions](krybot/SKIN_SOURCES.md).
