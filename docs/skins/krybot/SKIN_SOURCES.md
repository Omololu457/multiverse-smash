## Pixel and adaptation limits

Original SHA-256: `f6f58db89dd5c19f62839ab89038c8ec1e8c21052c0468309c576cb609c78c14`. Original RGB 655×680 file and all base gameplay assets remain unchanged. Reference downloads are research evidence; no pixels from the DS sheet or TV photos become gameplay frames.

`tools/krybot/skins/build.py` owns 28 anatomical region masks and finite RGB ramps. `region_mapping.json` records every pose's head, glove, boot, weapon and plate bounds, mapping, region counts and mask hashes. Shared gray is assigned by cell-local anatomy, never a global hue shift. The darkest outlines and ambiguous baked ground shadow remain exactly as supplied. Head aperture interiors retain their original dark pixels. Source highlights/shadows keep their ramp ordering. Existing rectangular pose masks approximate small material boundaries at this source resolution; they do not assert photogrammetric costume accuracy.

All three alternatives are honestly **palette adaptations**. DS cuff, wristblade, rounded shoulder geometry and proportions cannot be exact without new art; no shape is painted in. TV metallic reflectance and orange markings are translated into the source's limited pixel vocabulary. The supplied game blaster, shot and explosion art remain identical for every skin because no alternate effect palette was verified. No costume grants new combat behavior.

The TV still and production costume description carry the color evidence. Saved official YouTube thumbnails show Rangers and are not used as Krybot palette samples. DS colors were checked against the actual archived sheet (including the red miniature), not an unrelated fan recolor. Archive HTTP requests may return 406; a normal browser successfully loaded the actual sheet.
