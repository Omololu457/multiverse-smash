## Canon (Ranger Central)
- Bridge Carson is the S.P.D. Green Ranger (B-Squad). After Gruumm's defeat and Jack's departure he becomes the Blue Ranger.
- His genetic power is "able to track evil through psychic senses." His special skill is master mechanic.
- He is a computer genius, often underestimated, and quirky in ways that annoy the team. He relies on intuition more than any other Ranger and grows in confidence.
- Gear: Deltamax Striker, Delta Enforcer, Delta Morpher, Patrol Cycle. Zords: Delta Runner 3, S.W.A.T. Flyer 3.
- He was the first to sense something wrong with A-Squad.

## Adaptations (game mechanics, not canon claims)
- **Aura Read** is a short-range, cooldown-gated *mark* on one opponent. It is **not** mind reading. It grants no knowledge of future inputs, no invisibility detection and no lock-on through walls. It enables one bonus follow-up and nothing more.
- **Acrobat Step / Heel Return / Rising Arc** adapt the handstand and arc-kick cells on the supplied sheet. They are not specific on-screen techniques.
- **Deltamax Check** is a single Deltamax Striker shot.
- **Phase step** (double-tap teleport) was a brief requirement. It reuses SPD Blue's ground-teleport contract and is not a canon power.
- **Zord Assault** (ultimate) depends on the shared Ranger Zord system, which does not exist yet. No Delta Runner 3 art was invented. Tagline: "I had a feeling."
- Identity is kept separate: the id is `spd_green`. MMPR Green / Tommy Oliver and SPD Blue / Sky Tate are untouched.
