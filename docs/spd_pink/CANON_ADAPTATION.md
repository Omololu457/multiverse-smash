# S.P.D. Pink — Canon Adaptation

- **Syd Drew (B-Squad Pink):** her genetic power lets her hands take on the properties of a material she touches (Ranger Central character page). The game turns this into **Iron Reserve**: an explicit Charge that stores one "iron fist" for the next Heavy or Iron Breaker. She has no full-body metal armor, no invulnerability and no guard break.
- **Deltamax Striker** (the S.P.D. sidearm/baton): the Deltamax Shot and air shot use the sheet's own aim/fire cells, and the jab chain, Baton Reprisal and the Iron Barrage finisher are baton strikes.
- **Delta Runner 5** is her vehicle. It is *not* used for the ultimate: the shared Ranger Zord system is pending.
- **Ground phase step:** a gameplay adaptation that reuses SPD Blue's `groundTeleport.js`. It is not a canon power for Syd.
- **Skins:** "Inspired Palette" recolours (Alien X, Diamondhead, Upgrade, Heatblast, Chromastone, SPD Shadow). They are colour homages, not new art or other characters.
- **Sources:** Ranger Central (character page) was readable. The Power Rangers Fandom pages returned HTTP 402, so they were not used, and the Chromastone colour references were partial. The palette is an interpretation.
