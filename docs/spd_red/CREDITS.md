## Exclusions

**One** region of the sheet is excluded from the game, and it is excluded only because it is the ripper credit:

- **The WANTED poster's lower band** (sheet x 620–721, y 97–135) carries "ripped by the FRANKETE for TSR".
  - Ledgered as **excluded, credit**.
  - The scroll ornaments in that band are interleaved with the lettering, so the band is excluded whole rather than repainted.
  - The poster above it (the WANTED header and mugshot window) is used.

Everything else on the sheet is used: every frame of R1–R18, the poster top, and the gem. See `SPRITE_LEDGER.md`.
