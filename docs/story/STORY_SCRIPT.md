PROLOGUE

Multiverse Smash · The Other End of the Line

Record 01–36 • One number is one encounter. Opponents enter in listed order on the same health checkpoint.

A team slot enters as its full squad. A mirror opponent comes from the other side of the tear.

Shinjuku, ANBU, Shikai, Dark, Adult, Sensei, Seventh Hokage, Kamui, Fallen and Prime mark histories drawn through the rift.

Each returns to their living instant, however briefly. A meeting across histories leaves both histories intact.

Play the selected fighter's three intro lines once, before the tutorial. The player may be any fighter, including a team.

In a boss relay the final opponent holds the rift. On a loss, retry from the checkpoint.
