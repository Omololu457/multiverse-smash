Megumi Fushiguro (Jujutsu Kaisen) sprite sheet
==============================================
Sheet author : FinhJ
Edit/credit  : saxcreed (noted on the sheet)

Sliced into 15 per-action transparent strips. Background brown RGB(107,90,82)
keyed to transparency (JPG source, so a small tolerance + speck cleanup were
used). Section labels, the detached shikigami (Gama toad, Divine Dogs, Datto
rabbits, Nue, Orochi, Max Elephant/Bansho, Makora), the domain shadow, and the
"by saxcreed" note were excluded from the character strips. The Tokusa no Kage
Bojutsu rows share the same summon motion, each paired on the sheet with its
shikigami.
