  ITEM 3 — Goku's transform recolours his ATTACK art, not just locomotion. ✅ DONE
==========================================================================

THE PROBLEM
  enterGokuNextForm sets fighter._skinAnim = GOKU_FORM_ANIM[key], but GOKU_FORM_ANIM
  only overrides locomotion/state (idle/walk/run/jump/fall/dash) with real recoloured
  EB sheets. The ATTACK actions (light, heavy, up, air, down_air, gokuRush1-3,
  dragonFist, gokuKamehameha) have NO per-form art on disk, so they fell back to BASE
  (black-haired) art — Goku looked "un-transformed" the moment he attacked.

THE FIX  (placeholder tint, exactly the Piccolo Potential/Orange precedent)
  No per-form attack sheets exist, so — as the prompt directed — a per-form canvas
  palette-TINT recolours those attack frames toward the form colour, gated on
  fighter.transformIndex (1=SSJ gold / 2=SSG red / 3=SSBlue). CRUCIAL DETAIL: the tint
  is applied ONLY to the attack actions, NOT to idle/jump/dash (which already swap to
  real recoloured sheets) — otherwise the gold idle would be double-tinted. Verified:
  transformed IDLE filter stays "none"; transformed ATTACK filter = the form tint.
  This is a PLACEHOLDER pending bespoke per-form attack art (same status as Piccolo's
  deferred remodel) — commented as such in both files.
