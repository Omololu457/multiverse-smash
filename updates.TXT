3. Ben and Swampfire implementation

- Human variants retain AF/Classic/Prime movement and jump values across device
  setup, transform, revert, timeout and rematch; canonical one-jump state restores.
- Shared HP is preserved. Alien stats do not multiply cumulatively or leak into
  the human form. Prime requires development mode.
- Production selection exposes Swampfire only. Thirteen legacy aliens remain
  explicit development experiments with tinted human art; activation checks do
  not certify finished kits. Albedo stays disabled. Invalid old loadouts recover.
- Clockwork slowdown reaches the projectile hit pipeline and expires after 96
  simulation frames. Diamondhead eruption launches and resets juggle state.
- Energy Guard consumes one melee or projectile contact. Ordinary hit interruption
  invalidates pending Ben actions while preserving Superman's authored heavy armor.
- Projectile fields use explicit supported options and nullish defaults so zero
  and false survive. Swampfire melee block chip applies defense only once.
- Reset, KO, round and identity paths clear device transients and action epochs;
  detached shots persist. Vow randomness remains seeded and serializable.
- Swampfire uses the supplied 2611x3219 sheet, 30 row bands/250 source cells,
  labeled contact sheet, source ledger, 29 body strips and eight effect strips.
  Background keys preserve body palette; FX are separate and cosmetic.
- Swampfire modifiers: speed .92, damage 1.10, defense divisor 1.10, weight 1.15.
  Regrowth banks 35% actual eligible HP damage, waits 120 ticks, heals 2 per 10,
  and restores at most 80 per transformation.
- Normals, Flame Jet, Vine Eruption, Blaze Dash, Methane Inferno and Green Flash
  Roll are implemented through the shared input/combat systems. Exact move frame,
  range, damage, cost and timing conventions are in
  docs/ben10/aliens/swampfire/README.md.
- Four synthesized sound cues are placeholders; no voice pack is claimed. Walk
  reuses restrained run poses; emergence poses stand in for intro/victory. The
  contact ledger documents every source frame use/reuse/exclusion.
