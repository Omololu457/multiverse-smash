WHAT IT DOES
  At hit-connect the scene briefly desaturates toward stark B&W with an
  additive white flash centred on the impact point, then snaps back to
  colour as hitstop ends. Drawn OVER the scene/fighters but UNDER the HUD
  (HP bars + combo counter stay in colour). Layered WITH the existing
  per-tier hit-spark particles — never a replacement.

BASE INTENSITY — scales by hit tier (keyed off the hitstop weight):
  light 0.34 · heavy 0.50 · special 0.64 · ultimate 0.80 desaturation;
  duration 2–3 frames. A jab reads as a quick snap; an ult is cinematic.

COMBO ESCALATION (visual only) — mirrors the 5 / 9 / 15 combo-counter
  colour tiers. As the live combo climbs, each subsequent hit in the same
  combo flashes longer + more desaturated, and at 9+ / 15+ a ring of
  radiating white flash-lines streaks from the impact (denser + longer at
  15+). Combo hit 1 = base only; 15+ = noticeably more dramatic.

GATED OFF during a Brutality — both the trigger and the render bail on
  brutalityState.active, so it never clashes with a finisher.

IMPLEMENTATION — game.js ONLY, +93 lines, 100% additive. triggerImpactFrame
  only READS comboCounter + hitstop and sets a render-only impactFX object
  (fired on the combo rising-edge in updateComboDisplay); _drawImpactFrame
  renders it and ticks only its own timer, with a deterministic seed (no
  Math.random). NO writes to any damage value, combo-scaling constant,
  comboCounter, or hitstop state.

VERIFY — new harness/impact_frame_live.mjs (test:impact-frame 15/0): base
  single light hit, tier scaling light<heavy<special, combo sweep
  1/5/9/15, and the brutality-null gate. Render-only → determinism 9/0.

get wider than the world. NO-OP on ≤16:9 (cw/worldWidth ≤ static minZoom).
